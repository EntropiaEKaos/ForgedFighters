# RHEA — IRON CREW
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Pressure / weapon-assisted brawler  
**Combat:** boxing/brawling + compact battlefield technology

## Fantasy
A mechanic who turned tools of war into extensions of close combat. Weapons extend pressure; they do not turn her into a full-screen gunner.

## Silhouette
Powerful compact build, recognizable mechanical gauntlet/short weapon module, practical workwear/armor. Strong industrial shapes.

## Palette
Industrial metal, dark workwear and a hot mechanical accent distinct from rune/magic palettes.

## Animation language
Forward momentum, shoulder-led strikes, recoil integrated into combos, practical movement.

## Signature — Weapon Extensions
Short-range shots/boosts extend or alter strings with resource/heat constraints.

## Move pillars
Heavy jab strings, shotgun extension, booster advance, mechanical launcher/uppercut, close blast. Super: controlled arsenal sequence ending at brawling range.

## Weakness
Resource/heat and punishable overextension. Long-range firearm play remains intentionally limited.
