# ZAYN — STORM MONK
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Stance / technical  
**Combat:** disciplined martial arts + lightning

## Fantasy
Mastery through rhythm. Zayn changes intent rather than merely changing stats.

## Silhouette
Lean martial artist, wrapped forearms, flowing but short garments, distinct prayer/stance hand shapes. Electricity follows movement instead of covering him constantly.

## Palette
Storm neutrals with cool electric highlight; avoid Kael's crimson rune identity.

## Animation language
Exceptional pose clarity. Each stance has a recognizable guard and locomotion cadence.

## Signature — Three Stances
**Calm:** defense/counters. **Flow:** movement/conversions. **Storm:** offense/electric finishers. Transitions are actions with timing, not menu toggles.

## Move pillars
Palm strikes, kicks, parry, stance cancels, lightning finishers. Super: controlled storm sequence culminating in a precise martial strike.

## Weakness
Requires decisions/execution; wrong stance at the wrong time costs opportunity.
