# KAIRO — PLAGUE DOCTOR
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Trapper / setup  
**Combat:** alchemy, compounds, smoke and prepared zones

## Fantasy
Kairo wins the experiment before the opponent understands its ingredients. Clever interactions beat passive damage-over-time.

## Silhouette
Distinct physician/alchemist mask, compact coat, visible vials/tools. Avoid copying historical plague-doctor costume literally; build a Forged-specific combat silhouette.

## Palette
Dark laboratory materials with sickly-but-readable chemical accents. Different compounds use shape/icon cues in addition to color.

## Animation language
Precise throws, quick tool swaps, eccentric confidence. Setup animations must clearly expose vulnerability.

## Signature — Reactions
Limited compounds interact: cloud + catalyst, vial + strike, trap + forced movement. Favor combo/space effects over automatic health drain.

## Move pillars
Vial toss, ground compound, smoke, catalyst strike, trap activation. Super: controlled chain reaction across prepared compounds.

## Weakness
Needs preparation and inventory/resource decisions; weak when forced into unprepared scramble.
