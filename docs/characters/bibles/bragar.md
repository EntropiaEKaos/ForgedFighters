# BRAGAR — BLOODCLAW
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Berserker / aggression  
**Combat:** brutal claw fighting + progressive bestial state

## Fantasy
The match visibly pushes Bragar closer to the beast. Aggression is rewarded, but losing control creates risk.

## Silhouette
Heavy athletic humanoid with pronounced hands/claws and hunched predatory combat posture. Transformation evolves silhouette in readable stages.

## Palette
Natural dark materials, muted flesh/fur values and a restrained blood-energy accent. Avoid gore as visual identity.

## Animation language
Predatory stalking into explosive lunges. Higher Bloodlust increases animation intensity without changing core frame truth invisibly.

## Signature — Bloodlust
Deterministic meter built primarily through active combat. Thresholds unlock/modify defined options and visually intensify claws/posture.

## Move pillars
Lunge, claw rekka, maul command attack, anti-air swipe, savage pursuit. Super: temporary full bestial manifestation and decisive assault.

## Weakness
Linear approach tendencies and risk at high commitment. Bloodlust cannot become runaway passive snowballing.
