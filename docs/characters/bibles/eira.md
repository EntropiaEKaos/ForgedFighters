# EIRA — SPIRIT CALLER
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Puppet / summoner  
**Combat:** graceful martial movement + independently commanded spirit

## Fantasy
One player creates two threats. Eira and her spirit become beautiful—and frightening—when coordinated.

## Silhouette
Eira remains clean and readable beside a much larger spectral companion. Her garments suggest spiritual ritual without becoming visual noise.

## Palette
Dark natural base with luminous ethereal accent. Spirit uses translucency carefully; core silhouette must remain visible on every stage.

## Animation language
Eira is composed and economical; spirit movement is larger, animalistic and flowing. Their rhythms intentionally contrast.

## Signature — Spirit Companion
A deterministic secondary entity with explicit position/state/command rules. Initial visual fantasy: a great spectral wolf, subject to canon refinement.

## Move pillars
Spirit call, delayed strike, pincer setup, defensive recall, synchronized launcher, command reposition. Super: manifestation of a colossal ancestral spirit and synchronized finisher.

## Weakness
Opponent can disrupt spacing/coordination. Puppet state must have strong UI and no ambiguous ownership.
