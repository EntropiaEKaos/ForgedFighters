# DRAKON — VOID LORD
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Setplay / spatial zoner  
**Combat:** void rifts and displaced attacks

## Fantasy
Drakon attacks the assumptions of 2D space. He is threatening because position becomes a resource.

## Silhouette
Tall imposing figure with asymmetric void corruption/open negative shapes. Avoid generic horned demon design; the “missing space” motif is his signature.

## Palette
Near-black/deep cosmic values plus a restrained high-contrast void edge. Must remain readable against dark stages.

## Animation language
Unnaturally controlled motion, brief spatial discontinuities, deliberate stillness before impossible attacks.

## Signature — Void Rifts
Limited entrance/exit rifts redirect defined attacks or movement. All interactions have deterministic ownership, lifetime and collision rules.

## Move pillars
Rift placement, redirected projectile, displaced strike, space denial, teleport with commitment. Super: opens a massive controlled fracture that resolves into a clear finishing sequence.

## Weakness
Setup dependency and punishable rift management. No invisible/unreactable exits.
