# KAEL — THE RUNEBREAKER
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved baseline  
**Archetype:** All-rounder / rune fighter  
**Combat:** kickboxing + martial arts + runic amplification

## Fantasy
The benchmark fighter: immediately readable, versatile and increasingly dangerous as his runes awaken. Kael fights with disciplined physical technique rather than “casting spells”; rune energy amplifies the body.

## Silhouette & anatomy
Athletic ~1.85m frame, powerful shoulders/forearms, agile rather than bodybuilder-heavy. Asymmetrical broken armor and scarf/cloth movement create recognition at gameplay scale. Glowing arm runes are the primary read.

## Costume & materials
Graphite/black cloth, worn leather, dark broken metal armor. Avoid ornamental overload. Damage, seams and repairs suggest a fighter who survives battles.

## Palette & VFX language
Neutral charcoal/black + controlled crimson rune light. Rune geometry is angular/circular, sharp, ancient and fractured. Body sprite and VFX remain separate.

## Animation language
Grounded guard, efficient footwork, compact startup, explosive rune follow-through. Idle must communicate control, not constant exaggerated motion.

## Signature mechanic — Rune Charge
0 Base: dormant marks. 1 Focus: local glow. 2 Ascension: brighter lines + particles. 3 Rupture: unstable aura/trails. State is deterministic; VFX only represents it.

## Move pillars
Reliable normals; Rune Strike; Rune Wave; Rune Uppercut; Rune Grab; Rune Burst. Ultimate: **Apocalypse Breaker** — rune rupture/circle spectacle with a powerful physical finish.

## Presentation
Entrance: activates a rune while assuming guard. Victory: controlled release of excess rune energy. UI portrait: determined, direct gaze.

## Production locks
Readable runes; stable feet/root; no VFX baked into body frames; no visual state may alter combat rules.
