# SELENE — ARCMAGE
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Zoner / control  
**Combat:** arcane geometry, projectiles, seals and portals

## Fantasy
Selene designs the battlefield several seconds before the opponent realizes the trap.

## Silhouette
Tall elegant caster silhouette with one unmistakable arcane focus/gauntlet and layered asymmetrical garment. Keep hands visible because gestures communicate attacks.

## Palette
Dark refined base with luminous arcane accent distinct from Kael's crimson runes. Arcane shapes are precise, mathematical and complete—not fractured rune geometry.

## Animation language
Minimal physical waste; hand/arm gestures draw geometry. Calm posture contrasts with violent magical reactions.

## Signature — Arcane Seals
Places limited persistent seals used to alter routes, detonate zones or interact with projectiles. Hard cap and clear ownership indicators.

## Move pillars
Fast/slow projectile, seal placement, portal redirect, anti-air glyph, detonation. Super: temporary grand arcane array transforming spatial pressure without changing deterministic rules.

## Weakness
Poor close-range scramble and setup vulnerability. Portal exits and active seals remain legible.
