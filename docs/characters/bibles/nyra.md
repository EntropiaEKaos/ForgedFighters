# NYRA — SHADOW BLADE
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Rushdown / mobility  
**Combat:** blades + acrobatics + shadow movement

## Fantasy
The opponent never feels certain which side Nyra will attack from. Speed is precision, not random teleport spam.

## Silhouette
Lean compact athlete; low center of gravity; twin short blades; scarf/ribbons used sparingly for motion readability. Strong triangular/agile silhouette.

## Costume & palette
Layered stealth gear with lightweight protection. Near-black, deep indigo and a restrained luminous shadow accent. Avoid generic ninja clichés and excessive straps.

## Animation language
Catlike preparation, sudden acceleration, rapid recovery. Strong anticipation frames preserve competitive readability despite speed.

## Signature — Shadow Mark
Specific attacks mark the opponent. Mark enables controlled reposition/cross-up options with explicit rules and counterplay.

## Move pillars
Fast advancing normals, wall interaction, air mobility, blade rekka, feint, marked-step. Super fantasy: arena briefly falls into controlled shadow as Nyra executes a multi-angle assault.

## VFX
Thin shadow cuts, afterimages and short-lived geometric marks; never opaque enough to hide competitive information.

## Weakness/readability
Lower durability/range and commitment on failed entries. Teleports always have recognizable origin/destination language.
