# TORG — EARTHWARDEN
**Status:** Design Bible v1 — approved gameplay baseline; lore details revisable  
**Archetype:** Grappler / tank  
**Combat:** wrestling + brutal earth-powered strikes

## Fantasy
A walking fortress. Torg wins by occupying space, surviving the approach and making one correct grab terrifying.

## Silhouette
Largest broadest roster body. Massive forearms/hands and grounded legs. Rock/earth armor shapes grow from functional protection, not a full stone monster.

## Costume & palette
Weathered stone, iron and heavy natural fabrics; earth neutrals with mineral energy seams. Shapes must contrast strongly with Kael.

## Animation language
Deliberate weight, compression before movement, enormous impact recovery. Screen shake is presentation only and configurable.

## Signature — Stone Guard
Defined attacks gain controlled armor/absorption windows. It must never become unconditional forward invulnerability.

## Move pillars
Command grabs, anti-air grab, armored advance, seismic strike, corner carry. Super: rupture the arena floor and finish with a monumental throw.

## VFX
Dust, debris, stone fracture and localized shockwaves. Debris must not obscure silhouettes/hit confirmation.

## Weakness
Slow startup/mobility and punishable whiffs; strongest when the player earns proximity.
