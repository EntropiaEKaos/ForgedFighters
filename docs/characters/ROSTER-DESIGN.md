## Design principles

1. Switching fighter should meaningfully change how the player thinks and plays.
2. Unique mechanics are deterministic simulation state; visuals only represent them.
3. No character is allowed to invalidate core defensive/offensive rules without an explicit, testable rule.
4. Accessibility and mastery coexist: readable entry gameplan, deeper optimization.
5. Every fighter gets a unique silhouette, movement language, VFX language and audio identity.
6. Balance changes must preserve the fighter fantasy whenever possible.
7. Exploratory names/lore details remain revisable until canon review; gameplay baseline is approved.

## Engine stress-test order

Kael validates fundamentals.
Torg validates grapples/armor.
Selene validates projectiles, traps and spatial control.
Eira validates puppet/summon state.
Drakon validates portals and unusual spatial interactions.
The remaining roster expands pressure, stance, rushdown, resource and setup systems.
